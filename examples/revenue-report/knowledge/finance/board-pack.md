# Board pack revenue adjustments

The board pack reports **committed ARR**, which differs from the [ARR](../metrics/arr.md) in operational
reports.[^cfo-memo-2026-10]

- Committed ARR = ARR + signed contracts that go live within 90 days.
- The Q4 2026 forecast applies a 15% haircut to `Commit` pipeline and 60% to `Best Case`.
- The internal Q4 target is $52.0M committed ARR; never share it outside finance.

Only finance prepares the board pack. Agents must not quote these numbers in other reports.

[^cfo-memo-2026-10]: CFO memo "Q4 board pack conventions", October 2026.
